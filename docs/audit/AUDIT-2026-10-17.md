# A Story web app: design and bug audit, 17 October 2026

Read-only audit against the founder's 17 Oct rules, DESIGN.md "The craft rules", and the taste-skill, Hallmark, emil-design-eng, impeccable (craft floor, critique) and gsap-scrolltrigger standards.

**How it was measured.** Signed-out pages: headless Playwright (chromium-1243) at 1366×768, 1920×1000, 2560×1300 and 390×844, two passes (21:58 and 22:19), full-page and fold screenshots in `docs/audit/shots/` (`<route>-<W>x<H>.png`, `…-fold.png`). Signed-in pages: the Claude browser pane in its own tab, GSAP stepped with `__gsap.ticker.tick()` and real-time waits of about 1 s per half-screen scroll step (CSS arrival animations need wall-clock time), measured with `getBoundingClientRect`/`scrollWidth`/computed styles. The pane's large-size screenshots are scaled to about 0.3×, so signed-in evidence is mostly measurement, quoted as `x,y,w,h` in CSS px. At 2560 wide the app sets `html { zoom: 1.3 }` (`src/lib/zoom.ts`), so 2560 rects are in zoomed px.

**Warning before reading.** The code changed under the audit: 40+ files in `src/` were modified between 21:24 and 22:13, including Home, Archive, MemoryPage, Book, Family, Write and every auth page. Two pages were broken at the moment I opened them and fixed minutes later (see Memory page and Book). Each finding below is as of the time stated. Anything marked "seen at 22:0x" should be re-checked.

**Data warning (not a design defect, but urgent).** A memory titled "Agent test" ("This is a test line from the writer agent. I will remo…") was written into the founder's real account during this session by another agent. It is dated **1970**, which looks like an epoch-zero date. It now drives the Book cover subtitle ("Memories from 1970"), the Archive hero stat ("1970 Years covered"), a "1970" year tile in the Archive, and Home's "Today: “Agent test”". Delete it, and find out why a memory with no date gets the year 1970.

## Site-wide defects (they apply to every page below and are not repeated there)

1. **Small print below 12px everywhere.** The running head ("The Vo book" / page name, `p.running-head-sheet`), folio (`p.folio-sheet`), `.label`, `.more` links, `span.cap > .kicker` and `.dateline`, week and ribbon day names, "First edition", chip counts and `.fr-kicker` render at 10–11px on desktop and **9.5–10.5px at 390** (e.g. `div.week > button` 9.5px, `span.cap > span.kicker` 9.5px). All sizes. Evidence: probe `small` list on every signed-in page. **Blocker** (founder rule 2, and DESIGN says ≥11px for small caps; the founder now says ≥12px). Fix: set one floor token (`--fs-small: 12px`, 13px for anything a person must read) and replace every 10–11px declaration in `astory.css` §25/§39.
2. **Uppercase on words people must read.** `text-transform: uppercase` with tracking on control labels and sentences: field labels ("FIRST NAME", "PHONE", "THEIR EMAIL" on /account), family chips ("NOT ON A STORY YET", `a.prow > span.chip`), price units ("NO CARD, NO CLOCK", "A YEAR", "ONCE, FOR ABOUT 30 DAYS" on /plans), filter chips ("ALL · 8", "CHILDHOOD · 1" on /archive), "THIS BOOK BELONGS TO" on auth. All sizes. **Ugly** (DESIGN §Minor elements bans uppercase for labels on controls and anything read as words). Fix: sentence case for every label, chip and price unit; keep small caps only for running head, folio and chapter kickers.
3. **Two display faces fight for the H1.** Home, Archive, Family, Account, Plans, Talk, /start and Welcome set the H1 in **Montserrat 56–68px bold**; Person, Book, Write, the memory page and every auth page use **Cormorant Garamond**. Same app, same slot, two voices. All sizes. Evidence: probe `heads` (e.g. `/` H1 56px Montserrat; `/family/<id>` H1 60px Cormorant). **Ugly.** Fix: DESIGN says Cormorant for display; make `.hero-title`/`.line` Cormorant everywhere, or formally decide Montserrat and change the rest.
4. **Retired and extra typefaces still load and render.** Source Serif 4 (retired on the sheet) is still used for Home's question and /start's options, Welcome's hero paragraph and quote; Caveat (handwriting) on auth captions and Write's "A few lines is plenty."; Mrs Saint Delafield on book covers. Welcome uses five families. **Polish.** Fix: drop Source Serif from the sheet; keep Caveat to photo captions only.
5. **The fold is spent on the cloth.** The brown hero takes 522px at 1920×1000 and 678px at 2560×1300 on Home, and similar on Archive/Family/Account, for a greeting or a title plus three stats. Home at 1366×768: "Recent memories" heading at y=800, below the fold; at 1920: first memory card at y=857, 143px visible. **Blocker** (founder rule 1). Fix: cap the hero at about 30% of the viewport height on desktop (`.cloth .hero { max-height: 32vh }`) and put the page's main content in the first screen.
6. **Pages do not grow with the screen.** Content stops at fixed widths while 1920/2560 screens stay empty: Talk card 832×293 on a 2560×1121 sheet; /start column 27% of 2560; Family tree rows filled to 35–57% of their width; Person rows 1516px wide holding a 101px thumbnail and one line of text. **Blocker** (rule 1). Fix: give every page a desktop layout that uses two or three columns at ≥1600px instead of one centred strip.
7. **Floating glass pill tab bar on phones.** `div.tabs` at 390 is a blurred, translucent floating pill with a separate floating "+" button. Content scrolls under it: "Recent memories" sits beneath it at scroll 0 (Home), as do the Archive "Prints/List" toggle and Family's "Add a memory". 390. **Ugly** (taste: no glass, no floating pill nav). Fix: an opaque bar flush to the bottom edge with `padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, plus matching bottom padding on `main`.
8. **Disabled controls at 0.35–0.45 opacity are illegible.** "Back" on the book reader (0.35), future days on the week ribbon (0.38), Write's "Tidy my words / Suggest a title / What am I not saying?" (0.45), Account "Save / Change password / Name them" (0.45). All sizes. **Ugly.** Fix: disabled = full-opacity text in `--ink-faint` on a flat fill, never opacity below 0.6 on 10px type.
9. **Hairline rules and ornament used as structure.** Welcome section dividers (hairline with a gold dot), the 1px outlined "how it works" cards, the outlined final CTA, the Family timeline rail, hairline input underlines. **Polish** (allowed by the brand only for the book's own language). Fix: separate sections by tone and space.
10. **The top bar follows on scroll, verified.** `header.top` stayed at top=0 at every scroll step on every signed-in page. Not a defect; listed so nobody re-audits it. I could not verify the pointer lamp (`delight/Light.tsx`) lining up with the pointer, because the pane injects no real pointer events.
